Hi, I'm Varsha Yadav, a BCA student and aspiring web developer from Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh.

I'm pursuing my Bachelor of Computer Applications at Rajarshi School of Management and Technology, where I'm building a strong foundation in programming and computer applications. I enjoy turning ideas into clean, responsive websites and practical web applications.

I'm now looking for an internship or entry-level opportunity where I can learn from experienced teams, contribute to real projects and keep growing as a developer.
